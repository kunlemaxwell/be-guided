Lessons ask you questions as you go. The marks feed your journey and memory.

- **Guess first** asks a question before the answer. There are no marks; guessing first helps it stick.
- **Quick check** is a multiple-choice question in the middle of a lesson. Pick an answer to see if you are right. A wrong first try gives you a hint.
- **Quizzes** come after lessons and sections in a course, with a final quiz at the end. Some questions ask you to type an answer, and you get feedback on it.

Questions you get wrong are remembered, and Learn OTG brings them back in later lessons. See [Notebooks and memory](guide:progress/notebooks-and-memory).
